CLAIMIQ DEMO INSURANCE POLICY

Policy Name: SecureDrive Comprehensive Motor Insurance
Policy Number: DEMO-MOTOR-2026-001
Policy Type: Comprehensive Motor Insurance
Region: India

1. POLICY OVERVIEW

SecureDrive Comprehensive Motor Insurance provides financial protection for the insured vehicle against accidental damage, theft, fire, and certain natural disasters.

The policy is intended for demonstration and testing purposes only.

2. ACCIDENTAL DAMAGE

The policy covers repair costs resulting from accidental damage to the insured vehicle.

The maximum accidental damage coverage is INR 800,000 per claim.

The policyholder must report the accident within 7 calendar days from the date of the incident.

3. THEFT COVERAGE

If the insured vehicle is stolen and cannot be recovered, the policyholder may submit a theft claim.

The maximum theft coverage is INR 750,000.

The policyholder must provide a copy of the police complaint or FIR when submitting a theft claim.

The theft must be reported to the insurer within 48 hours after discovery.

4. FIRE DAMAGE

Damage caused by fire, explosion, or lightning is covered up to INR 600,000 per claim.

Damage caused intentionally by the policyholder is not covered.

5. NATURAL DISASTER COVERAGE

The policy covers vehicle damage caused by:

Flood
Cyclone
Earthquake
Landslide
Storm

Natural disaster coverage is limited to INR 500,000 per claim.

6. DEDUCTIBLE

A standard deductible of INR 5,000 applies to every approved accidental damage claim.

For natural disaster claims, the deductible is INR 10,000.

7. EXCLUSIONS

The policy does not cover:

Damage caused while driving under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs.

Damage caused while the vehicle is being used for illegal activities.

Normal wear and tear.

Mechanical failure that is not caused by a covered accident.

Damage caused intentionally by the policyholder.

Racing or competitive driving.

8. CLAIM DOCUMENTS

For an accidental damage claim, the policyholder should provide:

Completed claim form.
Vehicle registration certificate.
Driving licence of the driver.
Photographs of the damaged vehicle.
Repair estimate from an authorized service center.

For theft claims, a police complaint or FIR must also be provided.
